## Description (App Store)

**Stop organising. Start living well.**

Nobody wakes up wanting to organise their tasks. People wake up wanting a good day — time with people they love, health, not forgetting what matters, guilt-free downtime, and progress on meaningful goals.

One Pager is one daily page for a life well lived:

- **Today on Overview** — see your day at a glance: habits, teams, shows, plans, and people
- **Today Coach** — morning, afternoon, and evening check-ins woven with your chronotype and Pulse
- **Right now** — contextual nudges: walk before the match, cheer a partner, unwind without guilt
- **People in your circle** — optional partners who help you show up (private by default)
- **Sports, shows, events** — never miss what you care about; never feel behind on the rest
- **Recovery mode** — a softer day when life is hard

Organisation stays under the hood. You feel guided, not managed.

## TestFlight — What to Test (build notes)

### Story test focus

We’re testing a repositioning from “productivity app” to **“live well, one day at a time.”** Please react to language and flow, not just bugs.

1. **Onboarding complete** — Does “Live well, one day at a time” feel like the app you expected?
2. **Overview hero** — Does the tagline (“What would make today a good day?” etc.) resonate?
3. **Your day card** — Is the daily stack readable and motivating?
4. **Right now** — Do contextual nudges feel helpful or noisy? (partner cheer, walk before match, unwind)
5. **Today Coach** — Try “Get today’s check-in” morning/afternoon/evening. Is Pulse easy to find?
6. **Partners** — Share invite copy: “Live well together…” — would you send this to a friend?

### What we’re measuring

- Do users describe the app as **life coach / daily companion** vs **task manager**?
- Do they open Overview daily without feeling guilt?
- Do partner cheers / decision moments get tapped?

### Known scope

- Learning tab remains hidden until content is real
- IAP / Pro not yet wired in this build
